# Lumina – AI-Powered Mental Health Companion

## Overview

Lumina is an AI-powered mental health companion designed to provide emotional support through personalized conversations, voice interaction, and emotion-aware responses. The system combines conversational AI, speech recognition, and voice technology to create a more natural and supportive user experience.

Unlike traditional chatbots, Lumina focuses on creating meaningful interactions that help users feel heard, understood, and supported during moments of stress, loneliness, or emotional difficulty.

---

## Motivation

In today's fast-paced world, many people experience loneliness, stress, anxiety, and emotional challenges. While friends and family provide valuable support, they may not always be available when someone needs to talk.

The motivation behind this project is to leverage artificial intelligence to create a supportive companion that users can interact with anytime. By combining conversational intelligence with voice-based communication, the project aims to make technology feel more human, empathetic, and accessible.

---

## Target Users

This system is designed for:

* Students dealing with academic pressure and stress
* Individuals experiencing loneliness or emotional challenges
* Working professionals facing workplace stress
* People seeking a safe and non-judgmental space to express their thoughts
* Users who prefer voice-based interaction over traditional text communication

---

## Current Features

* AI-powered conversational support
* Voice-based interaction using Speech-to-Text
* Personalized conversations with relationship-based personas
* Emotion-aware response generation
* Text-to-Speech response playback
* User-friendly web interface built with Flask
* Support for both text and voice communication

---

## Future Enhancements

The following features are planned for future development:

* Voice cloning using uploaded voice samples of loved ones
* Multi-language support including regional languages
* Advanced emotion detection and mood tracking
* Personalized wellness recommendations
* Conversation history and user profiles
* Guided meditation and breathing exercises
* Mobile application support
* Real-time emotional analytics and progress tracking
* Integration with professional mental health resources

---

## Goal

The goal of Lumina is not to replace professional mental health services, but to provide accessible emotional support, companionship, and meaningful conversations whenever users need someone to talk to.
